  • We do not talk to your clients. They are yours.
  • We do not hand your team a tool and leave you to run it.
  • Under 100 videos a month we are the wrong call, and we will say so instead of taking the work.
How it works

Two calls, then it runs.

  1. 01 Book the call.

    Twenty minutes. Bring two real numbers: how many videos a month you produce, and what they cost you. Everything after this is decided by those two numbers.

  2. 02 We learn your style.

    Send ten videos you have already made. We extract the rules and rebuild them inside your Style System so you can see your own work come back out.

  3. 03 You compare.

    If the output is not yours, we stop there. The demo exists so you can judge this on your own material before committing to volume.

  4. 04 Setup.

    We build the Style System inside your dashboard and run the first production batch through it.

  5. 05 It runs.

    Weekly output, human QA on every frame, revisions until you approve. The per-video rate falls as your volume rises.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this going to look like our work, or like AI work?

Like yours. That is the entire product. The Style System is built from videos you have already made, so type, colour, captions, hooks and pacing are extracted from your own output rather than chosen by us. If it does not look like yours, the system has failed and we would rather you say so.

Is this AI?

Yes, underneath. You asked what it costs and how many videos it produces, so that is what we led with. There is a reasoning layer selecting the moments and applying your rules, and a person watching every frame before it ships. The architecture is our problem, not yours.

What if we do not like the output?

We rebuild until you approve. Nothing reaches a client without your sign-off, and the ten-video comparison exists precisely so you can judge this on your own material before you commit to a volume you might regret.

Do we lose the client relationship?

The opposite. Your clients never deal with us. We produce under your name, in your style, and you own the relationship and the dashboard.

What happens to our editors?

That is your call and we do not tell you how to staff your team. What usually happens is the repetitive cutting stops, and the same people spend their hours on the work that actually needs a person.

How long until we are live?

The setup is the slow part, because learning your style properly takes a few rounds. One format across one brand turns around quickly; six formats across twenty creators takes longer. We give you the dates before you commit, not after.

Under 100 videos a month. Should we even talk?

Probably not yet. At that volume the setup will not pay for itself and we would be selling you something that does not fit. Put your real numbers into the calculator above and it will tell you honestly, including when the answer is no.

Do you work with our competitors?

One production partner per niche. If we are already working with someone you compete with, we tell you on the call before you commit to anything.
